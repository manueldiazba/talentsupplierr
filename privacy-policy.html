How TalentSupplier (“we”, “us”) handles personal information on this website and in its search work.

Who we are

TalentSupplier is an independent executive search boutique based in Madrid, Spain, and the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: info@talentsupplier.eu.

What we process

Enquiries: the name, email address and details you choose to send us by email. Search work: professional information about leaders relevant to a mandate, taken from public professional sources or shared by you, such as your role history, experience and contact details. This website itself has no forms and does not collect personal data; our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs.

Purposes and legal basis

We use this information to answer enquiries, to carry out executive search mandates and to stay in touch about relevant opportunities. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a confidential search business, the steps needed to enter into or perform a contract with a client, and your consent where the law requires it. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for automated decisions.

Who sees it

Candidate information is shared with a client only as needed for a specific search, and only with the candidate’s knowledge. We use service providers for email and hosting, who process data on our instructions.

Transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to answer them and follow up. Search information is kept while it remains relevant to our work, and deleted or anonymised when it no longer is, or when you ask us to.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and stores nothing on your device. If we ever add analytics, it will load only after you agree to it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, or object to its use, by writing to info@talentsupplier.eu. We answer within one month. You may also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@talentsupplier.eu.
